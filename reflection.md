I used ChatGPT to identify policy topics and asked it to find a large volume of literature on those topics. In some cases, the links provided did not work, or the pages that opened were the homepages of official websites rather than the specific sources containing the data on teachers’ working hours that I required. I had to verify each piece of literature generated myself and retain only those from genuine and reliable sources.

In the initial draft, ChatGPT allocated 800,000 Australian dollars to workflow changes without providing any calculations. I questioned this figure and requested traceable sources. The revision linked the administrative support benchmark to the existing project budget in New South Wales and designated the 500,000 Australian dollar allocation for workflow changes as a provisional cap for the Victoria design project. These are not verified implementation costs for New South Wales. When generating relatively detailed content, AI may fabricate data and references in order to save tokens. This experience has made me more cautious about comparing figures with credible research before using AI-generated content.
